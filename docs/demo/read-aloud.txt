Three banks. One payment network. Each institution can see a fragment, but none
sees the complete cycle. What changes when the evidence connects? This is
FraudMesh. Follow the money. Reveal the network.

Start with Bank A. These are its contributed relationships. Switch to Bank B,
then Bank C: the same ring scenario, viewed through separate fragments. Now
reveal the network. The permitted signals connect into one cross-bank structure.
Each colored relationship retains its contributing institution. The movement
explains the change in visibility; the case comes from the running detector.

Select a relationship. Its supporting signal, contributing bank, hour bucket,
amount band and local transaction score are inspectable. Follow the reference
into the evidence explorer. Nothing here depends on an unexplained alert alone.
The local model scores transaction behavior. The graph rules identify structural
patterns. A separate policy combines that evidence into review priority. That
priority guides investigation; it is not a probability of fraud. Every case keeps
its supporting references and reason codes.

The shared scenario also includes legitimate payroll activity. Its fan-out
structure remains visible, but the policy does not recommend it for review.
Normal activity returns no case. These controlled examples show the distinction
between a structural finding and a recommendation; they do not establish
real-world accuracy.

Collaboration needs a defined boundary. The simulated bank stages prepare only
the permitted signal fields for the graph. Exact amounts and original references
stay in the local stage; the graph receives bucketed values and pseudonymous
relationships. Those tokens remain linkable, so pseudonymization is not anonymity.
Our deployment target is IBM LinuxONE: the architecture places local inference,
signal preparation and combined graph analysis in a reproducible Python workflow.
This recording is local; target execution and confidential computing require
separate verification. A configured server alone does not prove either claim.

The audit view checks the running service's hash-linked journal for consistency.
That is a local integrity check, not an immutable external record. Export the
case with its evidence and source labels, then return to the connected network.
FraudMesh gives an investigator a coherent structure, the signals behind it,
and a reason to review it. Three institutions. One connected investigation.
