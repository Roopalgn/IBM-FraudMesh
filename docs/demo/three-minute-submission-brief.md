# FraudMesh: three-minute submission brief

User-confirmed priority: the IBM Z Datathon 2026 demo video is the primary
deliverable. Its duration is 180 seconds. Aim for a submission that hooks judges,
demonstrates a distinctive working mechanism, and earns technical credibility.
This is a creative target, not a promise of winning or a verified scoring rubric.

## Core takeaway

Three institutions can each see a fragment. FraudMesh connects permitted signals
to expose a cross-bank structure and gives investigators the evidence to review it.

## Proposed 180-second cut

| Time | What the judge sees | What it must establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | The computed ring in isolated bank views; a direct opening line: “Each bank sees a fragment. The complete pattern crosses all three.” | The problem and the reason collaboration matters, before menus or team introductions. |
| 0:15–0:45 | Reveal the same scenario collaboratively; draw actual relationships and select its computed cross-bank case. | A memorable before/after grounded in the same inputs. Scope animation is not measured detection latency. |
| 0:45–1:20 | Select supporting signals; inspect contributor, bucketed time/amount, model scores, reason codes and review priority. | Working evidence and the specific roles of local ML, graph rules and the fixed triage policy. Priority is not a probability. |
| 1:20–1:45 | Show the legitimate payroll candidate and its lack of a review recommendation; briefly show the normal control. | The system distinguishes findings from recommendations in controlled examples. No broad false-positive claim. |
| 1:45–2:25 | Show the permitted sharing boundary and a concise architecture/execution view. If the workflow is verified on LinuxONE, show a real run and its platform evidence. | Why the execution platform and data boundary matter. Use only capabilities actually implemented and verified. |
| 2:25–3:00 | Verify journal consistency or export the evidence package, return to the connected case, and close on the investigator's decision. | A complete, reproducible investigation with a clear takeaway and concise prototype limits. |

## Direction for design and motion

Make the actual graph the visual protagonist. Use readable recording-scale type,
bank colors that remain consistent between shots, deliberate cursor movement,
and one authored reveal. Every animation must explain a relationship or state.
No fake streaming activity, fabricated business results, or implied verification.
Keep source labels visible. Supporting details can use captions or brief callouts;
the narration should carry the causal story rather than enumerate controls.

The product workspace remains an operational interface. The video is a curated
narrative through it, not a reason to add decorative effects across every screen.
